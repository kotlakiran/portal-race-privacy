# Portal Race Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 3, 2024**

Portal Race is an offline-capable game. This policy explains what the app
stores locally and what may be processed when optional online features are
enabled.

## Information processed

- **Local game data:** Game progress, profiles, settings, statistics, replay
  data, achievements, and rewards are stored on the device using Android
  Room and DataStore. This data remains on the device unless you choose an
  online feature or remove the app/data.
- **Account and cloud data (optional):** If you sign in, Firebase
  Authentication processes the account credentials and account identifier
  needed to authenticate you. Portal Race may store profile, progression,
  multiplayer match, and leaderboard data in Firebase Cloud Firestore so
  the online features work across sessions.
- **Diagnostics and usage analytics (optional):** Firebase Analytics may
  process aggregate gameplay and app-performance events, such as selected
  mode, match outcome, and coarse timing. Portal Race does not intentionally
  send raw game identifiers, email addresses, passwords, or FCM token
  values in analytics events.
- **Notifications (optional):** If notifications are enabled, Firebase
  Cloud Messaging processes a device registration token so Portal Race can
  deliver match and invitation notifications. The token is stored with the
  signed-in profile when required for that feature and is not displayed in
  the app.
- **Nearby multiplayer (optional):** Nearby Connections uses Bluetooth and
  local Wi-Fi capabilities to discover and communicate with another nearby
  device. Android may require Bluetooth, nearby-device, Wi-Fi, and (on older
  Android versions) location permissions. Nearby play is not required for
  local single-player play.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide authentication, cloud synchronization,
matchmaking, multiplayer, notifications, leaderboards, crash/performance
diagnostics, and aggregate product improvement. The app does not sell
personal information and does not use it for advertising.

## Retention and deletion

Local data can be removed by clearing Portal Race storage or uninstalling the
app. You can stop using online features by signing out. Cloud account and
associated game data can be deleted through the support contact published on
the Portal Race Google Play listing; requests are handled according to the
retention requirements of the services used. Notification registration can be
removed by disabling notifications or signing out.

## Children

Portal Race is not directed to children under the minimum age applicable in
their region. Do not create an account if you are not permitted to use the
online services.

## Contact

For privacy or deletion requests, use the verified support contact shown on
the Portal Race Google Play listing. The listing contact is the authoritative
support address for this app.
